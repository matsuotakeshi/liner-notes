Published articles, cross-board search and sequential reading
Withdrawals and hides conceal all descendants on every board; IDs and parent links remain. Receiving administrators/SIGOPs use HIDE locally; WITHDRAW is restricted to the origin BBS. RESTORE never reverses an origin withdrawal.
WITHDRAW id asks for confirmation; WITHDRAW id CONFIRM executes. The author, admin or responsible SIGOP (including inherited authority) may withdraw.
Withdrawal cannot be undone. IDs, replies and history remain. Physical body reclamation is a separate maintenance operation.
RETITLE id title lets the author, admin or responsible SIGOP rename an article in the current board; read and post permissions are also required.
FIND HERE|SUBTREE|ALL author-login|* keyword|* searches the current/subtree/all readable boards.
Examples: FIND ALL alice * / FIND ALL * FreeBSD / FIND HERE alice setup
+ fetches more results; a result number switches boards and reads the article.
SCAN [ASC|DESC] HERE|SUBTREE|ALL author-login|* keyword|* reads matching articles sequentially.
Enter or CONTINUE advances; STOP/Q stops. Reading does not mark articles read.
PAGER ON/OFF controls screens inside each article. Q during paging also stops the sequence.
INFO [board-id] displays board information.
