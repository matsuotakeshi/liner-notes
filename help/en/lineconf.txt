Line conference: persistent one-line messages in numbered rooms. Active members only.
LINECONF [ROOMS [AFTER]] lists rooms. LINECONF HISTORY ROOM [AFTER] lists saved lines.
LINECONF POST ROOM TEXT saves one UTF-8 line (maximum 512 bytes).
LINECONF NEW [ROOM [AFTER]] lists unread lines. Omit ROOM or use 0 for all rooms.
LINECONF POSITION ROOM shows your read marker. LINECONF SEEN ROOM ID marks all lines in that room through ID read. Use room 0 to mark the interval in all rooms.
Listing and failed storage operations do not mark lines read. Use NEXT from the output as AFTER for the next page.
LINECONF CREATE ROOM TITLE creates a room (administrator only).
