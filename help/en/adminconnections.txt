Connections, announcements and shutdown
CONNECTIONS: all connections, transport, activity, elapsed/idle time and traffic
DISCONNECT connection CONFIRM / ANNOUNCE text
SHUTDOWN seconds reason (1..86400) / SHUTDOWN CANCEL / SHUTDOWN NOW CONFIRM
In chat: /KICK login
USERLOG ALL / USERSTATS ALL

Global administrators only; SIGOP alone is insufficient. Announce, disconnect, shutdown and cancellation requests are recorded before execution. A recorded request does not prove execution succeeded; check the result as well.
